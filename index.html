Unboxing

Everything you need, right in the box.

What's included in the box?

The projector, a remote control, an HDMI cable, a power cord and an Egate quick-start guide — packed and ready to use the day it arrives.

Do I need any extra accessories?

No — plug it in and point it at a wall. A dedicated projector screen is optional and improves image quality further, but isn't required to get started.

Is a user manual provided?

Yes, every unit ships with an Egate-branded quick-start guide covering setup, remote functions and basic troubleshooting.

How do I clean the projector?

Wipe the lens and body gently with a dry microfiber cloth. Avoid liquids or harsh chemicals directly on the lens.
